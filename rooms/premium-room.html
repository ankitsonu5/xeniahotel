Room Description

Our Premium Room is spacious and elegantly designed with premium amenities for the discerning traveler. With 38 square meters of refined living space, this room features one king-sized beds with luxury mattresses and plush linens, making it ideal for families or groups of friends exploring the sacred city of Varanasi.

The room boasts a stylish red-accent interior design, a marble-finished bathroom with both a rain shower and bathtub, a dedicated work desk with an ergonomic chair, and a 50-inch Smart TV with premium channels. Enjoy complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi, a fully stocked Lunch or DINNER, and our signature 24-hour room service to make your stay truly memorable.
