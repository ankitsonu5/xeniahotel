Our Hotel

In the industrial and the spiritual capital of India, discover the perfect world of comfort and convenience at Hotel Xenia, the new boutique business hotel.

Desired and designed for the traveller, the hotel has spacious and aesthetically designed rooms with modern amenities. Connect to the world with free Wi-Fi throughout the hotel. Relax with the satellite TV channels or catch up with your business at our business center. Conveniently located just a distance of 1.5 km from Railway Station, 2 km from the Central Bus Stand and 12 km from the Airport.
